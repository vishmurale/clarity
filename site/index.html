The science

What is in the stick, and why.

Clarity is built around paraxanthine, the compound your body turns caffeine into. Here is what that means and what each part of the mix is there to do.

Paraxanthine, explained

When you drink coffee, caffeine does not stay caffeine for long. Your liver breaks it down, and most of it becomes paraxanthine. Smaller amounts become theobromine and theophylline.

Paraxanthine works the way caffeine does. It blocks adenosine, the signal that builds up through the day and makes you feel tired.

The catch with caffeine is that people convert it at very different speeds. Genetics, medication, and habits like smoking all change how fast the liver enzyme works. That is one reason the same cup of coffee feels smooth to one person and jittery to another.

Clarity skips the conversion step and gives you paraxanthine directly.
